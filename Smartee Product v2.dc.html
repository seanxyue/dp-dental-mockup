Smartee Aligners

Clear aligners for children, teenagers and adults

A series of clear, removable trays made for one patient’s teeth. Each set is worn for a set period, then replaced by the next, moving the teeth a small step at a time. Treatment is planned around the bite and jaw, with myofunctional exercises where needed.

Adults

FOR CHILDREN

Growth guidance for children

How the lips, tongue and breathing work at rest shapes how a child’s jaws and teeth develop. Smartee AG KIDS works on all three. While the jaws are still growing, treatment can guide how they develop as well as straighten the teeth.

  • 01Straightens teethClear aligners made for children
  • 02Guides jaw growthFront to back, up and down, side to side
  • 03Myofunctional trainersLip seal, tongue posture, nasal breathing
  • 04Medical referralENT or sleep physician if snoring or mouth breathing shows
TAKE THE CHILD SLEEP QUIZ +
SCREENING AND DIAGNOSIS

A dentist screens for clinical signs that may suggest a sleep-related breathing disorder; we do not diagnose one. A formal diagnosis is made by an ENT surgeon or sleep physician, usually with a sleep study. Our role is to recognise the signs early, guide growth where appropriate, and refer to the right medical specialist.

THE PROCESS

How treatment works

  1. 01ConsultationThe dentist examines the teeth, bite and jaw, and how the lips and tongue rest, then talks through the options.
  2. 02RecordsPhotos, X-rays and scans or impressions of the teeth.
  3. 03Treatment planThe tooth movements are planned digitally before treatment starts, with a 3D preview of the planned result.
  4. 04AlignersEach set is worn for a set period, then replaced by the next.
  5. 05ReviewsRegular check-ins at the clinic, with refinements if the teeth need further adjustment.
  6. 06RetainersWorn after treatment to hold the teeth in place.
