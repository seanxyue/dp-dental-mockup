What the score means

The score is the share of Yes answers among the questions answered Yes or No. Don’t know answers are left out. The cut-off, 0.33, is about one Yes in every three answers.

How it’s worked out

Yes answers ÷ (Yes + No answers)
8 Yes, 14 No8 ÷ 22 = 0.36Higher risk
  • Below 0.33, lower risk: a sleep-related breathing problem is less likely. Snoring on most nights, mouth breathing or daytime tiredness are still worth having checked.
  • 0.33 or above, higher risk: an assessment is the next step, which may include a referral to an ENT surgeon or sleep physician for a sleep study.

A higher score is not a diagnosis, and a lower score does not rule a problem out.

When snoring is worth checking

Occasional snoring, for example during a cold, is common in children. Snoring on more than half of nights, loud snoring, or pauses in breathing are worth having checked. Enlarged tonsils and adenoids, allergies and a blocked nose are common reasons for a child’s airway to narrow during sleep, and are assessed by an ENT surgeon.

  • 0 to 2, low risk: moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnoea is less likely. Ongoing tiredness or poor sleep is still worth raising with a doctor or dentist.
  • 3 to 4, intermediate risk: an assessment is a sensible next step.
  • 5 to 8, high risk: moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnoea is more likely. An assessment and a sleep study are the usual next steps.

Questions about the sleep quiz

Is the quiz a diagnosis?

No. STOP-Bang screens for risk. Obstructive sleep apnoea is diagnosed by a sleep physician or ENT surgeon, usually with a sleep study.

Why does it ask about BMI, age, neck size and gender?

Each is linked to a higher likelihood of obstructive sleep apnoea, so the questionnaire counts them alongside the sleep questions.

What if no one has seen the breathing pauses?

Answer No if it isn’t known. A partner or family member can help with the snoring and breathing questions.

What if there’s already a sleep study?

The quiz isn’t needed. Book a consultation and bring the sleep study report.

What happens to the email and phone number?

They are used to send the result and for DP Dental to follow up about it. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is there a quiz for children?

Yes. The child sleep quiz is for ages 2 to 17 and is filled in by a parent or caregiver.

Questions about the child sleep quiz

Is snoring normal in children?

Occasional snoring, for example during a cold, is common. Snoring on more than half of nights is worth having checked.

Who should fill in the quiz?

A parent or caregiver who knows how the child sleeps. Another adult who sees the child asleep, such as a grandparent or helper, can help with the night-time questions.

Why are there questions about attention and behaviour?

Poor sleep in children can show up as inattention, fidgeting or hyperactivity during the day, so the questionnaire includes them. They don’t diagnose ADHD or any other condition.

Does a higher score mean the child has sleep apnoea?

No. It means an assessment is worth doing. A diagnosis needs a sleep study arranged by a doctor.

What age is the quiz for?

Children aged 2 to 17. For 18 and over, there is the adult sleep quiz.

What does treatment for children involve?

It depends on the cause and is planned with the child’s doctor. Enlarged tonsils or adenoids are managed by an ENT surgeon. Dental care can include growth guidance and myofunctional therapy where appropriate.

What happens to the email and phone number?

They are used to send the result and for DP Dental to follow up about it. Details are in the privacy policy.

What Smartee aligners are

Smartee aligners are a series of clear, removable plastic trays made for one patient’s teeth. Each set is worn for a set period, then replaced by the next, moving the teeth a small step at a time. A dentist plans the treatment and reviews it throughout.

  • Clear and removable
  • Taken out to eat, brush and floss
  • Made for one patient’s teeth from their dental records
  • Planned and reviewed by a dentist at the clinic

How much does Smartee treatment cost?

The fee depends on the option a case needs and is confirmed after a consultation.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on how far the teeth need to move and, for children, on growth. The dentist gives an estimate at the consultation.

How many hours a day are aligners worn?

Most of the day and night, usually 20 to 22 hours. They are taken out to eat, to drink anything other than water, and to brush.

Will teeth need to be removed?

Treatment is planned with minimal extraction where clinically suitable. Whether any tooth needs removing is decided at the consultation.

Will the teeth move back after treatment?

Teeth can move after any orthodontic treatment. Retainers are worn afterwards to hold them in place.

Why start while a child is still growing?

While the jaws are still growing, treatment can guide how they develop as well as straighten the teeth. Whether starting early suits a particular child is decided at a consultation.

Can aligners treat snoring or sleep apnoea?

No. Snoring and breathing concerns need assessment by an ENT surgeon or sleep physician. Dental treatment can run alongside that assessment. See Sleep Apnoea & Snoring.

Is treatment reviewed in person?

Yes. Every stage is planned and reviewed at the clinic by a dentist.

What obstructive sleep apnoea is

During sleep, the muscles of the throat relax. In obstructive sleep apnoea (OSA), the airway narrows or closes for short periods, so breathing stops and starts through the night. Each time, the brain briefly wakes the body to reopen the airway, usually too briefly to remember. This breaks up sleep.

1 in 3adults in a Singapore community study had moderate to severe sleep-disordered breathing.Tan et al., Respirology, 2016

Common signs of sleep apnoea

Many people with sleep apnoea don’t notice the pauses in their breathing. A partner often notices first.

  • Loud, regular snoring
  • Pauses in breathing, or gasping or choking during sleep
  • Waking up tired after a full night’s sleep
  • Sleepiness during the day
  • Morning headaches
  • Poor concentration
  • Several trips to the bathroom at night

Is a sleep study needed first?

The diagnosis comes from a sleep study arranged by a sleep physician or ENT surgeon. If there isn’t one yet, a referral is made at the consultation.

What if CPAP hasn’t worked?

An oral appliance is one option to discuss with the sleep physician when CPAP isn’t tolerated.

Will an oral appliance stop snoring?

It’s designed to reduce snoring by keeping the airway more open during sleep. Results vary, and are checked with the sleep physician.

Does an oral appliance change the bite?

Some people notice changes in how their teeth meet over time, so the bite is checked at review visits.

What is it like to wear?

The appliance is custom-made and adjusted over several visits. It can take some time to get used to.

Can children have sleep apnoea?

Yes. Signs in children can include snoring, mouth breathing, restless sleep, bed-wetting, and tiredness or hyperactivity during the day. The Child Sleep Quiz is a starting point; a doctor makes the diagnosis.

            About Us

            Gentle, honest dentistry — for the whole family

            We're a team of dentists and care staff who believe a visit to the dentist should feel easy. That means clear advice, fair pricing, and the time to answer every question.

            Our story

            DP Dental started in 2006 as a small practice with one dental chair. Today we’re a boutique practice with two clinics in Orchard and Katong, eight chairs and a team of more than 40 people — providing ethical, evidence-based dentistry with an integrative, functional approach.

            We travel the world for up-to-date knowledge and skills, so we can solve problems at the root cause with the best minimally invasive options. Our Mandarin name, 诚芯牙医, sums up how we work: honest dentistry with a heart.

            Sustainability

            The first B Corp certified dental practice in Singapore and Asia

            Looking after people and the planet is part of how we run every clinic. Our core values come down to three things: people, purpose and planet.

            What B Corp means

            B Corp certification measures a business on more than profit. B Lab looks at five areas: governance, workers, customers, community and the environment. To keep the certification, we have to meet its standards for social and environmental performance, transparency and accountability.

            Dentistry uses a lot of materials, energy, chemicals and water, and produces a lot of waste. With Singapore aiming for net zero by 2050, we want to show that high-quality care doesn't have to cost the environment.

            Certified since November 2023 · Medical & dental practice · Singapore

            View our B Lab directory listing

            Three pillars

            We focus on prevention, which cuts down on costly treatment and its environmental footprint.
