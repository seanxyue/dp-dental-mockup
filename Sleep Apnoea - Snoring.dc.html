Loud snoring is often the first sign.

Obstructive sleep apnoea happens when the airway repeatedly narrows or closes during sleep. Breathing stops and starts, causing loud snoring and tiredness even after a full night in bed.

IS THIS YOU?
  • 01Snoring
  • 02Gasping for air at night
  • 03Several bathroom trips each night
  • 04Daytime sleepiness or fatigue
  • 05Poor concentration at work
  • 06Morning headaches

Often a partner notices before the person does. A sleep test with a doctor confirms the diagnosis.

HOW IT WORKS

A small appliance, worn only at night.

01

Holds the jaw forward

The appliance positions the lower jaw slightly forward while you sleep.

02

Keeps the airway open

With the jaw forward, the airway is less likely to narrow or close, which reduces snoring.

03

When CPAP is not tolerated

For people who find a mask and machine hard to sleep with, it is an option to discuss with the sleep doctor.

FOR CHILDREN

Snoring in children

Children should not snore regularly. Snoring, mouth breathing and restless sleep can affect how a child grows, concentrates and behaves, and are worth checking early.

  • 01Regular snoringMost nights, not only with a cold
  • 02Mouth breathingLips apart during the day or at night
  • 03Restless sleepTossing, waking or unusual sleeping positions
  • 04Daytime signsTiredness, poor focus or irritability

SCREENING AND DIAGNOSIS

A dentist screens for clinical signs that may suggest a sleep-related breathing disorder; we do not diagnose one. A formal diagnosis is made by an ENT surgeon or sleep physician, usually with a sleep study. Our role is to recognise the signs early, guide growth where appropriate, and refer to the right medical specialist.

THE PROCESS

How treatment works

  1. 01Screen and testA short questionnaire, then a sleep test and diagnosis with our medical colleagues.
  2. 02Fit the applianceA custom device is made from impressions of the teeth and fitted for comfort.
  3. 03Follow upWe fine-tune the fit, and add myofunctional therapy where it helps.
